Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

This application is a personal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app accesses basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the ability to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) for the owner's account only.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are used solely to publish videos chosen by the owner. They are stored in a private, access-restricted location and are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The app does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
